About Us

Building systems design rooted in clarity and constructability.

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. delivers coordinated mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection engineering for projects across New York.

Who we are

Established in 2000 and based in Floral Park, New York, HRM is a full-service consulting engineering firm. We work closely with clients, architects, and construction teams to deliver quality design, accurate assessments of existing conditions, and practical system alternatives throughout the project life cycle.

Mission

To provide coordinated, code-aware MEP and fire protection design that supports owners and design teams with clear recommendations, responsive communication, and documentation ready for construction.

Vision

To be a trusted engineering partner for New York’s built environment — known for integrated thinking, constructability, and a greener, more efficient approach to building systems.

Brief history

For more than two decades, HRM has supported residential, hospitality, institutional, commercial, healthcare, warehouse, and transit projects — from feasibility and schematic design through shop drawing review, site visits, and expediting services.

Certifications

HRM is a minority-owned firm aligned with public and private participation requirements.
