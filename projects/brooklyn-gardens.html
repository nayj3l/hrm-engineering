Brooklyn Gardens

105 Carlton Avenue, Brooklyn, NY

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided multidisciplinary engineering services for a full building infrastructure upgrade at Brooklyn Gardens. The scope included replacement of existing heating and cooling systems with a high-efficiency chilled water heat pump system, PTHPs for secondary spaces, and DOAS with energy recovery for ventilation. Plumbing upgrades included centralized domestic hot water, sanitary drainage, leak detection, domestic water booster systems, and gas service improvements. Fire protection work included sprinkler head replacement and a new standpipe system.
