NYCHA | Gaylord White Houses

2029 2nd Avenue, New York, NY

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided multidisciplinary engineering services for resiliency improvements at Gaylord White Houses. The scope included hydronic heating upgrades at the community center, PTHP system upgrades for the residential building, DOAS with energy recovery, electrical service and power upgrades, plumbing updates, and construction administration. Electrical resiliency work included relocation of the main electrical room above the flood zone and a new emergency generator.
