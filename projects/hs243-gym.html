HS 243 Gymnasium Upgrade

207-11 116th Avenue, Queens, NY 11411

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided MEP engineering services for the gymnasium upgrade at H.S. 243 Campus Magnet Reso A (Q490). The scope included new electrical distribution panels, power for telescopic bleachers, low-voltage devices, a local sound system, and new drinking fountains to support ADA requirements and the associated path-of-travel improvements.
