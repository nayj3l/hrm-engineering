IS 117 Bronx Reso ‘A’ Library Upgrade

1865 Morris Avenue, Bronx, NY 10453

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided MEP engineering services for the library upgrade at I.S. 117, including modifications to support a new architectural layout and recording room. The scope included new power and data receptacles, relocation and installation of low-voltage devices, new lighting and controls, power for new HVAC equipment, and installation of new split HVAC systems, exhaust registers, and air balancing to support the revised library layout.
