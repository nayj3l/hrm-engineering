HS for Arts & Technology Swimming Pool Upgrade

122 Amsterdam Avenue, New York, NY 10023

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection engineering services for the renovation and modernization of the swimming pool facility at HS for Arts & Technology (M490). The project involved upgrades to critical building systems serving the natatorium and associated support spaces, requiring detailed coordination among multiple engineering disciplines and stakeholders.

The scope of work included evaluation of existing conditions, development of engineering design solutions, preparation of construction documents, and coordination of building systems required to support the upgraded facility. Attention was given to the unique environmental requirements associated with natatorium facilities, including ventilation, humidity control, occupant comfort, system reliability, and long-term maintainability. The project required careful integration of new systems within an existing educational facility while maintaining compliance with applicable codes, standards, and owner requirements.
