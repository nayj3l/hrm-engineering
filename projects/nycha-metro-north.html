Residential

NYCHA | Metro North Plaza

304 E 102nd Street, New York, NY

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided multidisciplinary engineering services for the alteration and improvement of three residential buildings at Metro North Plaza. The work included existing conditions investigations; conversion of the existing steam heating systems to hydronic heating; ventilation upgrades using DOAS with energy recovery; electrical power coordination; and plumbing system updates. HRM also supported construction documentation, construction administration, and coordination with the owner and project stakeholders.
