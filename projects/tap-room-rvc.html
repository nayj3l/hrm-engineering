Tap Room – Rockville Centre

47-51 North Village Avenue, Rockville Centre, NY

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided HVAC, plumbing, and electrical engineering services for the interior alteration of an existing Tap Room restaurant. The work included upgrades to the cellar kitchen, first-floor bar and dining areas, and additional dining space on the second floor. Design included HVAC and ventilation systems, domestic water, sanitary, vent, grease and gas piping, and electrical power, lighting, and controls.
