PS90Q Fire Alarm System and Ventilation Upgrade

86-50 109th Street, Richmond Hill, NY 11418

HRM Engineering Services provided mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering services for the heating and ventilation upgrade at PS90Q. The project involved modernization of existing HVAC systems to improve indoor air quality, occupant comfort, and system reliability throughout the facility. Work included replacement of heating and ventilation equipment, ventilation improvements, and associated electrical and plumbing modifications required to support the new systems.

The project required extensive coordination within an occupied educational facility, including consideration of construction phasing, operational constraints, and stakeholder coordination to minimize disruption to school activities.
