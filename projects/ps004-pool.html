P.S. 004 – Bronx Swimming Pool Upgrade

1701 Fulton Avenue, Bronx, NY 10457

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection engineering services for the swimming pool upgrade project at P.S. 004 – Bronx (X004). The project involved the modernization and improvement of critical building systems serving the natatorium and related support spaces within the school facility. The assignment required comprehensive evaluation of existing infrastructure and the development of coordinated engineering solutions to support the renovation of the facility while maintaining compliance with applicable codes, standards, and owner requirements.

The project included extensive coordination among multiple engineering disciplines to ensure the successful integration of mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection systems within the existing building. Consideration was given to the specialized environmental and operational requirements associated with natatorium facilities, including ventilation, humidity control, system performance, energy efficiency, and long-term reliability. The work was performed in an active educational environment and required careful planning, coordination, and communication with project stakeholders throughout the design and construction process.
