Penn Station LIRR Renovation Phase 2

1 Penn Plaza, New York, NY

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided mechanical and HVAC engineering and Revit modeling for the Penn Station LIRR Train Hall Phase 2 project. The scope included LIRR back-of-house spaces, temporary crew quarters, the NYCT 33rd Street entrance, elevator machine rooms, and communication rooms. Design included energy-recovery water-source heat pump systems, VAV distribution, BMS/DDC controls, post-fire smoke purge, emergency-generator fuel-oil systems, and dedicated equipment-room cooling.
