PS 279X Playground Redevelopment

2100 Walton Avenue, Bronx, NY 10453

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided electrical and plumbing engineering services for the redevelopment of the school playground at P.S. 279X (X279). The electrical scope included power and controls for new outdoor lighting, new receptacles, power for motorized garage doors, and a new lighting control panel utilizing the existing electrical distribution system. Plumbing work included outdoor drinking fountains and modification of existing plumbing connections as required.
