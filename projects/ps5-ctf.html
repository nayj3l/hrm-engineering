PS 5 CTF Room Conversion

564 Jackson Avenue, Bronx, NY 10455

HRM Engineering Services, P.C. provided MEP engineering services for the conversion of existing space into new classrooms at PS 5 (X005). The scope included new power and data receptacles, lighting and controls, power for new HVAC equipment, new HVAC equipment and exhaust systems, extension of branch ducts, and air balancing to provide the required airflow for the newly partitioned spaces. Plumbing and sprinkler fixtures were also relocated as required by the revised layout.
